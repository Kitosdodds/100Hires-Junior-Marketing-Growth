## Post 1: AI Content Engineering at Ahrefs
- **Date:** May 2026
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/thinkingslow/
- **Topic:** Announcing the Claude Code content engineering system

### Key points
- Built 23 skill files that mirror the Ahrefs editorial process
- Pipeline runs from keyword to publish-ready draft in 6–12 minutes
- Published ~15 new articles and ~30 updates using this system
- Each skill file handles one editorial step: topic analysis, briefing, outlining, drafting, line editing, internal linking, metadata
- Team members can fork the pipeline and customize for their own writing style
- Explicitly states they have no plans to use AI to scale content volume — goal is maintaining quality

## Post 2: AI content quality has crossed the threshold
- **Date:** March 2026
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/thinkingslow/
- **Topic:** The quality gap between AI and human content has closed

### Key points
- Argues that "great writing" is more mechanical than people think — it follows repeatable principles
- LLMs apply these principles more consistently than fatigued human writers
- Infrastructure improvements (Claude Code, MCP, agentic workflows) matter more than raw model improvements
- Content marketing is "intensely formulaic" — informational SEO content follows a recipe LLMs can execute
- Still acknowledges a gap between a skilled writer using AI and a layperson prompting ChatGPT, but says this gap is closing fast

## Post 3: 74% of new web pages contain AI content
- **Date:** May 2026
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/thinkingslow/
- **Topic:** Ahrefs internal study on AI content prevalence

### Key points
- Analyzed 900,000 newly created web pages in April 2025
- 74.2% contained AI-generated content (detected via internal "bot_or_not" classifier)
- Implication: the market is flooded with AI content, so differentiation through quality and original data is now critical
- This data supports the argument for depth over volume

# Ross Simmonds — LinkedIn Posts

## Post 1: Create once, distribute forever
- **Date:** 2025–2026 (recurring framework)
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/rosssimmonds/
- **Topic:** Content distribution as the primary growth lever

### Key points
- Most teams spend 80% of effort on creation and 20% on distribution — should be inverted
- Every article should be repurposed into at least 3 formats: LinkedIn post, short video, community post
- Discovery now happens across LinkedIn, Reddit, YouTube, and AI assistants — not just Google
- A great article nobody sees is worth nothing

## Post 2: Reddit as a B2B channel
- **Date:** 2026
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/rosssimmonds/
- **Topic:** Reddit's growing role in B2B discovery and AI citations

### Key points
- In 2018, Reddit was dismissed as a marketing channel; in 2026, it powers over 60% of bottom-funnel LLM citations
- Reddit threads are heavily cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini
- For B2B SaaS, authentic participation in relevant subreddits creates both direct traffic and AI visibility
- This is an earned channel — astroturfing gets caught and backfires

## Post 3: OpenAI and HubSpot media acquisitions
- **Date:** May 2026
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/rosssimmonds/
- **Topic:** Why tech companies are acquiring media properties

### Key points
- Both OpenAI and HubSpot made media acquisitions in the same quarter
- Not about content quality — about owning distribution channels
- Reinforces his thesis: distribution is leverage, and leverage compounds
- Practical takeaway: build your own distribution channels before someone else owns the attention

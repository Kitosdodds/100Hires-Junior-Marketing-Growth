## Post 1: AirOps State of AEO 2026 findings
- **Date:** June 2026
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/schwartze/
- **Topic:** Key data from the AirOps Answer Engine Optimization report

### Key points
- 85% of brand mentions in AI answers come from third-party pages, not the brand's own site
- Pages updated in the last 3 months are cited 3x more than stale content
- Clean, sequential heading structure (H1 → H2 → H3) drives 2.8x higher citation rates

- Conclusion: most SEO audits still measure rankings — that metric is increasingly incomplete

## Post 2: Middle-funnel content is where the ROI lives
- **Date:** 2025–2026 (recurring theme)
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/schwartze/
- **Topic:** Shifting content production toward commercial intent

### Key points
- Top-of-funnel keywords are increasingly answered by AI directly, reducing click-through rates
- Bottom-of-funnel is pay-to-play (dominated by ads and aggregators)
- Middle-funnel content (comparisons, use-cases, workflows) is where SEO still delivers ROI
- Example: helped a LatAm e-commerce company shift from generic "what is a sink?" content to focused "best compact garage sink" queries — direct ROI improvement

## Post 3: Great AI content beats bad human content
- **Date:** 2025
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/schwartze/
- **Topic:** The quality debate around AI-generated content

### Key points
- Google does not care whether content is written by a human or an LLM — it cares whether users find it helpful
- The binary "AI content is bad" take is outdated — quality depends on the process, not the tool
- Simple rule for startups: if you are not already running paid marketing to establish benchmarks, SEO is premature
- AI is a copilot, not an autopilot — strategy still requires human judgment

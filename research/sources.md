# Research Sources: AI-Powered SEO Content Production

**Topic:** How real teams use AI to produce SEO content that ranks (and gets cited by AI search engines) without producing generic slop.

**Selection criteria.** I filtered for practitioners over commentators. Each person on this list either runs content production at a real company, runs an agency doing it for clients, or builds the tools used for it. I skipped the names that dominate "top SEO experts" listicles unless they publish their actual process, data, or case studies. I also aimed for a mix of vantage points: in-house, agency, consultant, and tool founder, since each sees a different part of the problem.

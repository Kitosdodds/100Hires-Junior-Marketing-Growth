# Ross Simmonds — YouTube Transcript Notes

## Video 1: Content Distribution in the Age of AI (Zero Click Conference)
- **Expert:** Ross Simmonds
- **Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXxFJAg7YJw
- **Published:** October 2025
- **Collected:** August 2, 2026

### Summary
Conference talk at the Zero Click event on how AI changes content distribution strategy for B2B marketers.

### Key takeaways
- Core framework: Repurpose, Remix, Reshare
  - Repurpose: transform content across formats (webinar to blog post to downloadable guide to newsletter)
  - Remix: change the consumption experience (extract audio for Spotify, cut a 5-minute segment for social)
  - Reshare: only 20% of your audience saw it the first time — resharing serves the 80% who missed it
- In B2B, video remains high value because selling is still human-to-human
- Most teams celebrate publishing and then move to the next piece — distribution is where ROI compounds
- Permission to stop chasing new content and start maximizing what you already have

## Video 2: SEO Week 2026 Talk
- **Expert:** Ross Simmonds
- **Source:** https://seoweek.org/ross-simmonds-2026/
- **Published:** March 2, 2026
- **Collected:** August 2, 2026

### Summary
Presentation on how discovery extends beyond Google and why distribution creates leverage.

### Key takeaways
- SEO visibility increasingly comes from Reddit, LinkedIn, YouTube — not just organic Google results
- Content performs when placed inside existing conversations, not just published on your own blog
- Holistic SEO (brand, PR, social signals) outperforms pure onsite optimization
- Reddit was dismissed in 2018; in 2026, it powers over 60% of bottom-funnel LLM citations
- Low-risk tests combined with selective high-risk bets help teams learn faster and build competitive advantage

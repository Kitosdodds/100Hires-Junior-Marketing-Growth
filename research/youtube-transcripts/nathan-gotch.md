# Nathan Gotch — YouTube Transcript Notes

## Video 1: The AI SEO Checklist I'd Use in 2026
- **Expert:** Nathan Gotch
- **Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0FmEV0jE5TQ
- **Published:** July 14, 2025
- **Collected:** August 2, 2026

### Summary
A comprehensive checklist combining traditional SEO techniques with GEO (Generative Engine Optimization). Designed as a step-by-step guide for practitioners.

### Key takeaways
- AI search visibility is now a measurable KPI: track whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini cite your content
- Recommends cross-referencing AI-suggested keywords against Google Search Console data (high impressions, low clicks)
- Checklist format: meant to be printed and followed step-by-step
- Covers both on-page optimization and AI-specific optimization in one workflow
- Emphasizes measuring results in revenue, not just traffic

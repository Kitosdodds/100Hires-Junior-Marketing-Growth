# Eli Schwartz — YouTube Transcript Notes

## Video 1: Product-Led SEO in AI Era (Surfer Webinar)
- **Expert:** Eli Schwartz
- **Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x5CgYCRLgbc
- **Published:** April 29, 2026
- **Collected:** August 2, 2026

### Summary
Webinar discussing how product-led SEO strategies must evolve as AI changes user search behavior.

### Key takeaways
- SEO strategy should be embedded in the product roadmap, not bolted on after launch
- User behavior is evolving: people search differently when they expect AI-generated answers
- Organic growth is most durable when it comes from features and content users genuinely need
- The keyword-centric, algorithm-chasing playbook is no longer delivering results
- Focus on understanding real user needs rather than trying to outsmart algorithms

## Video 2: SEO's New Playbook (SearchPilot Webinar with Will Critchlow)
- **Expert:** Eli Schwartz (with Will Critchlow)
- **Source:** https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/blog/seo-playbook-survive-ai-product-led-growth-eli-schwartz
- **Published:** May 23, 2025
- **Collected:** August 2, 2026

### Summary
Discussion on why the traditional SEO playbook has stopped working and what replaces it.

### Key takeaways
- AI has changed how people search — intent and context matter more than keyword matching
- Product-led SEO means building content that serves the product journey, not just capturing search traffic
- Companies that treat SEO as a product feature (not a marketing tactic) have more durable growth
- Simple hiring test for SEO: if a startup is not already running paid marketing to establish benchmarks, SEO investment is premature

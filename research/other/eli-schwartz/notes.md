## Source 1: "How to do SEO right in 2025" (GTMnow newsletter)
- **URL:** https://thegtmnewsletter.substack.com/p/how-to-do-seo-right-in-2025-hint
- **Published:** 2025
- **Collected:** August 2, 2026

### Key takeaways
- Simple rule: if a startup isn't already running paid marketing, he won't take them on for SEO — because without benchmarks, there's no way to calculate ROI
- Google doesn't care if content is written by a human or an LLM — it cares whether users find it helpful
- Paraphrased: great AI content beats bad human content every time
- Target the middle of the funnel — top-of-funnel is saturated (and now AI-generated), bottom-of-funnel is pay-to-play, but middle-funnel is the goldmine
- Example: helped a LatAm e-commerce company move from fluffy "what is a sink?" content to focused "best compact garage sink" content — direct ROI improvement

## Source 2: "SEO's New Playbook" (SearchPilot webinar with Will Critchlow)
- **URL:** https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/blog/seo-playbook-survive-ai-product-led-growth-eli-schwartz
- **Published:** May 23, 2025
- **Collected:** August 2, 2026

### Key takeaways
- The keyword-centric, algorithm-chasing playbook is no longer delivering results
- AI has changed how people search — intent and context matter more than keyword matching
- Rather than trying to outsmart algorithms, focus on understanding real user needs
- Product-led SEO: SEO strategy should be embedded in the product roadmap, not bolted on after launch

## Source 3: LinkedIn post on AirOps State of AEO 2026 report
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/schwartze
- **Published:** June 2026
- **Collected:** August 2, 2026

### Key takeaways
- 85% of brand mentions in AI answers come from third-party pages, not the brand's own site
- Pages updated in the last 3 months are cited 3x more than stale ones
- Clean, sequential heading structure drives 2.8x higher citation rates
- Schema markup: only 61% of cited pages carry 3+ schema types, but that lifts citation odds by 13%
- Conclusion: most SEO audits still measure rankings — that's the wrong metric now

## Source 1: "Content Distribution in the Age of AI" (Zero Click conference)
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXxFJAg7YJw
- **Published:** October 8, 2025 (recorded), November 13, 2025 (published)
- **Collected:** August 2, 2026

### Key takeaways
- Core argument: most teams stop at pressing "publish" and celebrate — distribution is where the real value is
- Framework: Repurpose, Remix, Reshare
  - Repurpose: turn content into different formats (webinar → blog post → downloadable guide → newsletter)
  - Remix: change the consumption experience (audio from webinar → Spotify; best 5-minute segment → social clip)
  - Reshare: if only 20% of your audience saw it the first time, resharing serves the 80% who missed it
- In B2B, video is still worth the investment because selling is still "human to human"

## Source 3: SEO Week 2026 talk
- **URL:** https://seoweek.org/ross-simmonds-2026/

### Key takeaways
- Discovery extends beyond Google: SEO visibility increasingly comes from Reddit, LinkedIn, YouTube

- Experimentation drives advantage: low-risk tests and selective high-risk bets help teams learn faster
